The Ivy Lee Method

Focus, Prioritize, Deliver. Inspired by the Ivy Lee Method, this app helps you brainstorm and then ruthlessly narrow your daily focus to your top 6 tasks (adjustable).

Add any extra tasks you plan to do today if time allows, or move them to the backlog to avoid overwhelm and ensure your most important work gets done.

Today View

Focus Section: Your top 6 tasks (adjustable) for today. The counter next to the heading (for example 4/6) shows how many of your daily goal you have finished. The tasks are numbered: drag to set the order and always start with number 1. Click the number to remove a task from focus.

Other tasks: Additional tasks you plan to do today if time allows.

Swipe Actions:
• Click or swipe LEFT to mark as done. The task stays on your list, struck through, for the rest of the day and moves to the Archive tomorrow. Click ✓ again to undo.
• Swipe RIGHT to move to backlog
• Drag UP/DOWN to reorder within sections

Other Actions:
• Click to add a task to Focus (click its number to remove it)
• Click to edit
• Click to delete

Backlog View

Tasks you want to do, but not today. Keep your backlog organized without cluttering your daily focus.

Actions:
• Swipe LEFT to move back to Today's Tasks
• Click to edit
• Click to delete

Archive View

Finished tasks move here the day after you completed them. Look back at what you have delivered.

Actions:
• Swipe LEFT to move back to Today's Tasks
• Click to edit
• Click to delete

Settings

Max focus tasks: Set how many tasks can be in your Focus section (1-10, default is 6).

New-day review: The first time you open the app on a new day you decide for each unfinished focus task: keep it in focus or move it to the backlog. It works the same after a weekend.

Theme: Choose between light and dark mode.

Export data: Download all your tasks, backlog and archive as a JSON file for backup.

Pro Tips

  • Keep your Focus section to truly important tasks only
  • Don't be afraid to move tasks to backlog - it's better than feeling overwhelmed
  • Fill your focus list the evening before, so tomorrow starts with a clear plan
