    How it works

    • Choose what matters. Pick up to 6 tasks for today and do them in order, starting with number 1.
    • Everything else waits. Add it under "Everything else", or send it to Later. Nothing is lost.
    • Finish it. Done tasks move to Done tomorrow. Unfinished focus tasks are reviewed each new day.
    • Buttons and swipes. Every swipe has a button too. Swipe left to finish a task or add it to your focus, right to send it to Later.
    • Your data stays on this device. Use Settings → Export to keep a backup.
